# Nothing reports a child that outlives SIGKILL.

both process shells

Codex's `finishTermination` discards the boolean from `closesWithin`, Pi's now
does the same, and the manager swallows dispose rejections — so the one
condition DESIGN worried about is the one condition no layer would tell you
about. Making `kill()` record or throw on a false return is the fix.

## Close note

Absorbed by OW-sozopu, which replaced the three process shells with one `ChildProcessShell` in `src/server/adapters/child-process.ts`.
Its escalation no longer drops the second `closesWithin` result: a child not closed `KILL_GRACE_MS` after SIGKILL fires `onExit` once with a "did not close within ...ms of SIGKILL" error (test in `child-process.test.ts`), and `docs/DESIGN.md`'s "What the wrapper chain does to process events" records it.
What this card also said — that no layer tells you — is still true one layer up, since every caller of `kill()` has let go of its listeners and the session manager swallows dispose rejections; that is OW-tozuyo.
