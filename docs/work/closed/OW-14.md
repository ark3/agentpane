# `close()` flags a startup as torn down but leaves the record in `#attaching`, so a re-attach arriving before that startup settles joins it and inherits its rejection — a 404 for a session that is on disk.

`session-manager.ts`

Transient, and a retry works; the joiner should skip a torn-down record instead.

## Close note

Superseded by OW-bulanu (landed 2026-09-29), which made one function, `#retire` in `src/server/http/session-manager.ts`, the only place a startup leaves `#attaching` and `container.starting`, and has `close()` retire the startup it flags in the same synchronous run.
A re-attach after a close can no longer find and join the torn-down record.
Tests pinning it, each red on the previous `main`: "starts afresh for an attach that follows a close of a session still in its index lookup" and "starts afresh for an attach that follows a close of a session whose start outlives its disposal".
